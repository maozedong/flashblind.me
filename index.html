FAQ

How to make Instagram profile picture glow?

Use Flashblind to convert your image to HDR. Upload the PNG to Instagram without applying filters.

Why are some avatars so bright?

They use HDR images that can display much brighter highlights on HDR-capable screens.

Does Instagram support HDR photos?

Yes, when uploaded without filters. Filters often strip HDR metadata and reduce the glow.

What is an HDR PNG?

It is a PNG with an HDR color profile (Rec.2020 PQ) so HDR screens can show brighter highlights.

What devices can see HDR glow?

HDR screens like iPhone X+, MacBook Pro (2018+), MacBook Air (M1+), iPad Pro, and flagship Androids.

How to Upload Your Glowing Avatar

Instagram

  1. Download the PNG from Flashblind
  2. Open Instagram and go to Edit Profile
  3. Upload the image as your profile picture
  4. Do NOT apply any filters — filters strip HDR metadata

X / Twitter

  1. Download the PNG from Flashblind
  2. Go to Settings → Profile → Edit
  3. Upload as profile picture or header
  4. Save without editing — the glow is preserved automatically

Threads

Threads uses your Instagram profile picture. Update it on Instagram and it syncs automatically.

TikTok

TikTok compresses profile pictures and removes HDR. For the glow effect, use the image in your video content instead — HDR works in TikTok videos.
