Quick steps

  1. Upload a photo to Flashblind.
  2. Boost brightness until the preview glows.
  3. Download the HDR PNG and save it to Photos.
  4. Upload to Instagram as your profile picture.
  5. Do NOT apply filters — filters strip HDR metadata.

FAQ

Why does it glow? HDR avatars can display brightness beyond normal images on HDR screens.

Where will it glow? iPhone X+, modern iPads, MacBooks, and most flagship Androids.

Will it work on all devices? On SDR screens it looks like a normal image.
