How to Make an X (Twitter) Avatar Glow

X supports HDR avatars on bright screens. Flashblind makes the conversion in your browser.

Make my avatar glow

Quick steps

  1. Upload a photo to Flashblind.
  2. Adjust brightness until the preview glows.
  3. Download the HDR PNG.
  4. Upload to X as your profile photo or header.
  5. Avoid edits/filters — they can strip HDR metadata.

FAQ

Why does it look brighter? HDR images can exceed standard brightness levels on HDR displays.

Where will it glow? iPhone X+, MacBook Pro (2018+), and most flagship Androids.

Will it look normal on older screens? Yes, it will look like a regular image.
