Upload Tips: Keep the Glow

The #1 way to lose the glow is “helpful” apps and editors. Upload this file the right way and stay blinding.

Upload Tips

  • Use the Original PNG. Upload the exact file generated by Flashblind.
  • High Brightness. Keep brightness high to verify the effect.
  • Direct Upload. Upload the file directly without intermediate processing.
  • No External Editing. Avoid editing the file in other software before uploading.
  • Wait for Processing. Some services may take time to process the high dynamic range data.
  • Check Compatible Screens. View the result on an HDR-capable screen.
  • SDR Fallback. Remember that SDR screens will display the standard version.

What Breaks HDR

  • Screenshots. Taking a screenshot will strip glow data instantly.
  • Filters. Applying filters or effects will strip glow data.
  • Resizing. Manual resizing or cropping often removes essential data.
  • Format Conversion. Converting to JPG or other non-HDR formats prevents the glow.
  • Compression. Aggressive compression algorithms can remove brightness data.

Upload Flow

  1. 1Generate your image in Flashblind.
  2. 2Save the PNG file directly to your files.
  3. 3Select the file in the upload interface.
  4. 4Confirm upload and view on a supported screen.

FAQ

Why doesn't it glow?

The glow requires an HDR-capable screen. SDR screens will show the normal image.

Can I use filters?

No, applying filters typically strips the glow data required for the effect.

Is it visible everywhere?

The effect is only visible on screens that support and display High Dynamic Range content.

Why upload the PNG directly?

Flashblind generates a specialized PNG. Editing or re-saving it often destroys the brightness data.
